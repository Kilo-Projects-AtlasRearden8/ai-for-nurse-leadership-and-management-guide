## Quick reminder: AI ground rules

```
1. No patient names, birth dates, MRNs, or any protected health information.
2. No staff names or personal information.
3. AI drafts, humans decide. Read everything before you use it.
4. AI never directs patient care or makes clinical judgments.
5. AI never makes hiring, discipline, or scheduling decisions.
6. Unsure? Ask the nurse manager or educator before you use it.
```
