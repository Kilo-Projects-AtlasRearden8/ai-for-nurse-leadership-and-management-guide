# Meeting Prep: Using AI to Prepare for Meetings

Meetings take up a lot of a nurse manager's time. AI can help you prepare faster — drafting agendas, talking points, anticipated questions, and follow-up notes — so you can spend more time with your team and your patients.

**Reminder: This is education only. Do not paste confidential staffing details, patient information, or personnel matters into AI tools.**

---

## How AI helps with meeting prep

| Meeting task | How AI can help |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Agenda drafting | Turn a rough list of topics into a timed agenda |
| Talking points | Turn your notes into clear speaking points for each agenda item |
| Anticipating questions | Suggest questions staff might ask, so you can prepare answers |
| Pre-read summaries | Summarize long policies or reports into key points |
| Follow-up notes | Turn messy meeting notes into clean action items and owners |

## Step-by-step meeting prep workflow

**Step 1: Write your goal in one sentence.**
Before you open AI, decide what the meeting needs to accomplish.
- Example: "Decide how the unit will cover the two upcoming staff vacations."
- Example: "Share the new fall-prevention protocol and get staff input."

**Step 2: Draft the agenda.**
Give AI your goal, the meeting length, and a rough topic list. Ask for a timed agenda.
- Example prompt: "I'm leading a 30-minute staff meeting on our unit's new fall-prevention protocol. Draft a timed agenda with: welcome (2 min), protocol overview (8 min), staff questions and concerns (12 min), next steps (8 min). Keep the tone friendly and direct."

**Step 3: Draft talking points.**
For each agenda item, ask AI to turn your notes into 2–4 bullet points you can speak from.
- Example prompt: "I need to tell staff that charge nurses will now do safety huddles after every admission. Draft 3 short talking points I can read at a staff meeting."

**Step 4: Anticipate questions.**
Ask AI what staff might ask, so you're not caught off guard.
- Example prompt: "Staff might push back on post-admission safety huddles because they're busy. List 5 likely questions or concerns and one short, honest answer for each."

**Step 5: Prepare pre-reads.**
If you're sending staff a policy or report before the meeting, ask AI to summarize it into 5 key points so staff can skim it.

**Step 6: Plan your follow-up.**
After the meeting, paste your (de-identified) notes into AI and ask for a clean list of action items, owners, and due dates. Always read the output and correct anything wrong before sending it.

## Example: prep for a unit council meeting

**Goal:** Review last quarter's medication-error near misses and agree on one improvement to pilot.

**Agenda AI might draft:**
1. Welcome and purpose (5 min)
2. Near-miss data review (10 min)
3. Staff observations (10 min)
4. Pick one improvement to pilot (15 min)
5. Next steps and owner (5 min)

**Talking points AI might draft for "near-miss data review":**
- Near misses rose slightly last quarter, mostly around insulin dosing at shift change.
- No patients were harmed. We want to keep it that way.
- We're not looking for blame — we're looking for one system change to try.

**Anticipated questions:**
- "Will this mean more paperwork?" Answer: "No — one checkbox added to an existing form."
- "Who decides if we keep the pilot?" Answer: "The unit council reviews data in 60 days."

## Do's and don'ts

**Do:**
- Use de-identified examples ("a patient on 3 East" rather than names or MRNs).
- Read everything AI produces out loud before you use it in a meeting.
- Keep your own notes — AI is an assistant, not your memory.
- Save your agendas and notes where your unit normally stores them.

**Don't:**
- Paste staff names, performance issues, disciplinary matters, or salaries.
- Paste patient information of any kind.
- Ask AI to evaluate staff performance or pick who gets what shift.
- Send AI-generated meeting minutes without reading them first.
